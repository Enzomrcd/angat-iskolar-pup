The ANGAT Iskolar PUP (AIP) Membership Drive is a recurring organizational development and volunteer recruitment initiative conducted twice within every one-year term of the organization. Strategically scheduled at the opening of each academic semester, the project aims to recruit, engage, and develop aspiring Volunteers ng Bayan who share the organization's commitment to community service, social responsibility, environmental sustainability, and people-centered development.

As an organization committed to community engagement and people-centered initiatives, ANGAT Iskolar PUP recognizes the importance of establishing a systematic and sustainable membership development process. The organization aims to provide aspiring volunteers with an environment where they can learn through actual community involvement, understand the realities of different sectors, and develop a deeper appreciation of their roles and responsibilities as Volunteers ng Bayan.

The Membership Drive is usually conducted twice within the organization's one-year term, with the first recruitment cycle taking place from March to May and the second from August to October. Applicants from across the PUP System will undergo a probationary period, during which they will be assigned to their respective sectors and expected to actively participate in, organize, and implement meaningful projects and activities that respond to the needs of the people and the community.

Through this process, the organization seeks to recruit, develop, and empower socially conscious student volunteers through meaningful community engagement, experiential learning, and leadership development. It also aims to strengthen the organization's internal capacity by cultivating a new generation of committed volunteers who can contribute to the implementation of responsive and sustainable community initiatives.
