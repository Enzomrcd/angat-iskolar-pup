The Community Learning Project (CLP) is an educational initiative spearheaded by the ANGAT Iskolar PUP - Committee on Education, usually conducted twice within the months of September and December. It aims to extend the organization's commitment to promoting accessible and inclusive education by providing meaningful learning opportunities to different communities, particularly those with limited access to educational resources and learning support.

The project seeks to engage various beneficiaries, including children, adults, and Persons Deprived of Liberty (PDLs), through educational discussions, interactive learning activities, literacy initiatives, and other community-based educational programs. These activities are designed to respond to the different learning needs and circumstances of the intended beneficiaries.

This initiative provides ANGAT Iskolar PUP volunteers with opportunities to share their knowledge, develop their teaching and facilitation skills, and gain meaningful experiences in community-based education. It encourages volunteers to recognize their role in making education more accessible while developing a deeper understanding of the educational challenges experienced by different sectors of society.

Ultimately, the Community Learning Project upholds the belief that education is a fundamental right and an important instrument for individual empowerment and community development. Through this initiative, ANGAT Iskolar PUP seeks to bring learning beyond the classroom and reaffirm its commitment to making education accessible, inclusive, and meaningful for everyone, regardless of age, background, or circumstances.
