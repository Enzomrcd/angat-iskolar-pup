The Coastal Cleanup Drive is an environmental initiative spearheaded by the ANGAT Iskolar PUP - Committee on Environmental and Agricultural Development, conducted annually every September in celebration of the International Coastal Cleanup Day. The project aims to promote environmental awareness and encourage active volunteer participation in protecting coastal and marine ecosystems against pollution and improper waste disposal.

The initiative is implemented in partnership with the Manila Department of Environment and Public Services, bringing together student volunteers and environmental advocates to participate in organized coastal cleanup activities. Through this partnership, the organization seeks to strengthen collaboration with local government agencies and contribute to existing environmental protection and waste management efforts.

The project involves the collection and proper segregation of waste materials found along designated coastal areas, particularly plastics and other forms of non-biodegradable waste that threaten marine biodiversity. Beyond the actual cleanup, volunteers are also encouraged to understand the environmental consequences of marine pollution and the importance of responsible waste management and sustainable practices.

This Coastal Cleanup Drive aims to cultivate environmental consciousness, collective responsibility, and active participation among student volunteers and the wider community. Through this initiative, ANGAT Iskolar PUP reaffirms its commitment to environmental protection and recognizes that safeguarding our coastal and marine resources requires sustained community involvement and collective action.
