Brigada Eskwela is a community service initiative originally spearheaded by the Committee on Environment and now adopted by the Committee on Environmental and Agricultural Development, in collaboration with the Committee on Education. The project aims to extend assistance to nearby elementary schools by providing volunteer support in preparing, maintaining, and improving school facilities to ensure a clean, safe, and conducive learning environment for learners.

The initiative involves the participation of ANGAT Iskolar PUP volunteers in various school preparation activities, such as classroom cleaning, organizing learning spaces, maintaining school surroundings, and assisting in other necessary preparations identified by the partner schools. Through this collaboration, the organization seeks to support school management and contribute to the collective effort of preparing educational facilities for the opening of classes.

Brigada Eskwela is usually conducted at the beginning of the Department of Education's annual Brigada Eskwela campaign, typically in June or July. Beyond providing physical assistance, the project promotes volunteerism, environmental responsibility, and educational solidarity by recognizing that creating a conducive learning environment is a shared responsibility among schools, students, and communities.
