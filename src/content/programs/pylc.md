The PUP Young Leaders Congress (PYLC) is an annual flagship project of ANGAT Iskolar PUP, typically conducted during the BER months— September to November —of every calendar year. It is a leadership development and student empowerment initiative that brings together aspiring and current student leaders from the Polytechnic University of the Philippines (PUP) and other educational institutions across Metro Manila.

The development of effective and responsible young leaders is essential in building responsive institutions, empowered communities, and a progressive society. Students and young individuals occupy an important position in shaping the present and future of the country. Their participation in organizational leadership, community development, and civic engagement provides opportunities to influence decisions, initiate solutions, and contribute to meaningful social transformation. 

By bringing together aspiring and current student leaders from different educational institutions, the Congress promotes an environment where knowledge is shared, experiences are valued, and collective initiatives are encouraged. It provides a platform for student leaders and young individuals to connect, exchange ideas, share experiences, and develop the necessary competencies to become effective, responsible, and socially conscious leaders. 

Through a series of leadership discussions, educational sessions, interactive activities, collaborative workshops, and networking opportunities, PYLC seeks to strengthen the leadership capabilities of its participants while encouraging meaningful partnerships among student organizations and youth formations.
