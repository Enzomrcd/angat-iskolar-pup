Sintang Paskuhan is an annual Christmas holiday project of ANGAT Iskolar PUP, conducted every December, that aims to express appreciation, gratitude, and solidarity with the hardworking personnel of the Sintang Paaralan, particularly the security guards, janitorial staff, and other university service workers.


The project seeks to recognize their invaluable contributions in maintaining a safe, clean, orderly, and conducive environment for the entire Sintang Paaralan community. Through a gift-giving activity, the organization aims to share the joy of Christmas by providing simple gifts and tokens of appreciation to these workers in recognition of their dedication and everyday service.


Anchored on the values of generosity, compassion, and solidarity, the initiative encourages volunteers to extend kindness and recognize the people whose efforts often go unnoticed. It also provides an opportunity for student volunteers to strengthen their appreciation of the different sectors that contribute to the university community and promote a culture of gratitude and genuine service.


This project aims to make the Christmas season more meaningful for the university's hardworking personnel by reminding them that their efforts, sacrifices, and contributions are valued. Through this initiative, ANGAT Iskolar PUP hopes to foster a stronger sense of community, togetherness, and the spirit of giving within the Sintang Paaralan.
