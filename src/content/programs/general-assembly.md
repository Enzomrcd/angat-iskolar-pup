The ANGAT Iskolar PUP General Assembly is a gathering of all members of the organization, serving as a platform for organizational engagement, collective learning, and solidarity among its Volunteers ng Bayan. It aims to strengthen the members' sense of belonging, reinforce their shared commitment to the organization's principles and objectives, and provide an avenue for everyone to reconnect, interact, and collectively envision the direction of ANGAT Iskolar PUP.

The assembly features various activities, including team-building, educational discussions, and solidarity speeches, designed to strengthen camaraderie, encourage the exchange of ideas, and deepen members' understanding of the organization's advocacies and community engagements. These activities also provide opportunities for volunteers to share their experiences, express their perspectives, and learn from one another as members of a common organization.

Ultimately, the General Assembly seeks to foster a stronger sense of unity, solidarity, and collective responsibility among all ANGAT Iskolar PUP members. By bringing together volunteers from different committees, the assembly aims to cultivate a unified organizational identity grounded in one shared vision: to serve communities, empower people, and advance meaningful community initiatives through genuine volunteerism, collective action, and solidarity.
